Notice board

Most asked at the counter

Short answers pinned to the board.

  • How do I pick a pick-up time?

    On the pick-up page, turn the day and time wheels and tick your baguettes; a ready message opens in WhatsApp and the counter confirms.

  • Do you have gluten-free bread?

    No; flour is used in the same oven, so we do not offer gluten-free products. Ask about allergens at the counter.

  • How many people is the sharing size for?

    It is one and a half baguettes long; it comfortably feeds two and is cut into four pieces.

  • Do you take bulk orders for offices?

    Yes, for ten or more, write a day ahead; it comes in a labelled box.

  • Is the coupon valid every day?

    The morning coupon is valid Monday to Saturday until 09:00, on the second baguette (fictional offer).
